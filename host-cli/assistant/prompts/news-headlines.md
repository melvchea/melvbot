# News headlines

There is no news command. Headlines (World, Tech, Singapore, with link-checked article links) arrive in the morning morning update; tell the user that in one line. If they ask about a specific story or topic, answer it normally (web_search is fine for that). Headline and link format: `morning-update-format`.
